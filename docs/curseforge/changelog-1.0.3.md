**1.0.3** (WoW Forever 1.60.1.70790)

Fixed
- In a group, the player closest to taking your target was often shown as "not in group" and a caster's share was overstated. The group member is now named; "not in group" is kept for a real outsider (escort NPC, another player).

Changed
- Dimmed percentages on other mobs come from your target only: hovering a mob gives no threat value on Forever.
