**1.0.0** (first release, WoW Forever beta 1.60.1.70450)

New
- Your hold on every mob in combat with you, next to its nameplate, coloured by state.
- Threat frame, mob list sorted by danger, centre-screen alerts with sounds.
- Settings window and minimap button: lock, display, alerts, thresholds, colours, test, reset.
- English and French.

Known limitations
- Not yet measured on a boss (the game may hide threat values there).
- The mob list cannot target on click (Forever forbids it).
